Build your AI video editor

O Reel Forge grab a video raw — with cuts, silences, and mistakes — and returns a professional reel: clean cuts, motion graphics, real B-roll, captions, and SFX. Without you opening a video editor.

But it's not a ready-made editor: it's a meta-skill that interviews you and generates YOUR editor, with your identity. This course opens up the engine and teaches you how to use it.

The engine in 5 phases: raw footage comes in on the left and a reel comes out on the right—each blue box is an automated step.
